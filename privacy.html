Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Cobblekin does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app keeps

The game saves your progress (which levels you have cleared, the bonus pennants you have earned and which story pages you have seen) on your device only. It never leaves your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • No network connections of any kind.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
