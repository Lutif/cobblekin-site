Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue and say which level (for example, chapter 2, level 4) and what happened.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag a Cobblekin near the structure and let go: it ties itself on to the nearest friends. Build until the structure reaches the chimney, and the rest walk home.

What is a bonus pennant? Each level has an extra goal. Rescue at least that many Cobblekin to earn the level's pennant.

Why won't a Cob tie on? A Cob needs at least two friends within reach. Puffs need only one.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
